Kalaignar has splendidly created and given us this rare historical novel, "Romapuri Pandiyan." In it, in his own graceful Tamil style, he has described the trade relationship that existed between Tamil Nadu and Rome, and the ancient civilization and valour of the Tamils. We can see that this novel has been shaped in a way that sustains its flavour and gives readers a sense of excitement.

Our thanks to Tamilkani Pathippagam and to Kalaignar for granting us permission to publish this major novel!

Our heartfelt thanks also to the Tamil public, who have accepted this book and continued to encourage us, and to the printers who printed it with enthusiasm!

Pazha. Chidambaram  
Bharathi Pathippagam
