"Romapuri Pandiyan" is now being published again. My thanks to Bharathi Pathippagam, which has obtained the rights for this edition from Tamilkani Pathippagam and is bringing it out!

When this novel was released in 1974, Mozhignayiru Devaneya Pavanar, who presided and delivered the principal address; Chief Justice Ananthanarayanan, G.C.S., who released the book and delivered the special address; and Kaviyarasu Kannadasan, who received the first copy and spoke with affection, were all with us. They are no more today! Their speeches, which appeared in the first edition, are included in this edition as well. They have a place in my heart too. May their fame live on!

With affection,  
M. Karunanidhi

Chennai
