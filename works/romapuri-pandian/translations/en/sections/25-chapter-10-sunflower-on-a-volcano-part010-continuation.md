"Forgive me. It is only right to think, 'What if we were placed in a situation like this...' brother," Thamarai said pleadingly.

"A situation for us! Mad girl, must that situation befall us only from now on? What is our situation now? Think of it, Thamarai! Think! Where is our fortress that rose high enough to touch the clouds? Where are the battlements? Where is the sight of your brother seated in state in the royal hall? And toward these enemies who caused all of it to vanish—pity, compassion, tenderness, sympathy!" Irungovel became a volcano.

"Karikala Cholan is our enemy, isn't he, brother? What enmity is there between us and the Pandiyans?"

Before Irungovel answered Thamarai's question, his lips quivered. He ground his teeth.

"You say the Pandiyan is not an enemy? If not for this sinner here, Karikalan's life would by now have flown away on the wind—wasn't it he who stopped that? Not only that, Thamarai; here is some new news for you! In another day or two, the Pandiyan army is going to encircle us in this forest. They plan to lay siege with a great force, arrest me, you, and the royal family, and demand that all these people be released."

—Before he had finished saying this, Thamarai began to tremble. More than the fear caused by the news that the Pandiyans were going to invade, she was frightened that her brother was revealing that news right in front of Sezhiyan. But that fear soon disappeared. She concluded that even putting the news into Sezhiyan's ears was part of her brother's statecraft.

Irungovel went to Sezhiyan, patted him on the back and said, "Impassioned young man! The power to resolve the crisis that has arisen for you and me is now in your hands. Are you asking what crisis? You will not ask! You are a man capable of understanding! Peruvazhuthi

<!-- source boundary: scan 151 → scan 152 -->

Pandiyan's forces may surround us, and if they do, we certainly cannot escape. We are not in a position to proclaim that no one can defeat us.

But one thing. The one life for whose rescue the Pandiyan invasion is about to take place—that life they certainly cannot save. That life is yours! When all of us are surrounded by the Pandiyans, do you think we will return you alive to them? Who would? Don't you know? So there is only one way for you to escape alive—I will tell you; say at once whether you can carry it out or not!" He delivered a whole speech.

Eager to learn what exactly Irungovel wanted him to do, Sezhiyan asked, "What way?"

"Nothing extraordinary. You must send a palm-leaf message to Peruvazhuthi Pandiyan. That is all." Irungovel uttered these words with a little gentleness.

"What message?"

"A marriage message!"

"A marriage message!"

"Yes—and a palm leaf carrying the news that you are going to be married."

"I don't understand!"

"I will make you understand. You yourself must write it. Do you know the important lines that must be included in the palm leaf to the Pandiyan?"

"What are they?"

"Your Majesty, the Pandiyan King! I have heard that you are going to gather a great army to rescue me. Through this palm leaf I inform you that there is no longer any need for you to rescue me. I have now become Irungovel's close ally. Through this relationship, the Pandiyan Empire becomes my enemy."

"Ah! What did you say?"

"Don't be alarmed, Sezhiyan! Listen to all of it! The pleasant news is only now coming."

<!-- source boundary: scan 152 → scan 153 -->

"Enough—enough—do not pour honey into my ears; please go away, King—go away!"

"Listen to this too, brother! O Pandiyan King! Do you wish to know why enmity has grown in me toward you? You arranged my marriage and then suddenly stopped it; what followed from that has now broken our relationship. The woman you once showed me in a portrait—I have now seen her here in Irungovel's house, in person. Don't you know what happens after seeing her? Irungovel is now my brother-in-law; yes, Your Majesty! It has been decided that I shall marry Thamarai, the lamp of the Velir clan!"

Irungovel's words shocked both Sezhiyan and Thamarai. They both stood staring at him. Is it really my brother who is speaking.... Thamarai wondered. Did her brother truly have such an idea? Or had he decided to turn Sezhiyan into a traitor merely to escape the present danger? She could not understand anything. But she did realize one thing: her brother was handling some piece of statecraft very skillfully.

Sezhiyan was utterly confused. He stood there after hearing those unexpected words. Did Irungovel really want to give him his sister in marriage and form a family alliance? Or was he plotting to win by deceiving both him and the Pandiyans? What if Irungovel genuinely came forward of his own accord to give Thamarai to him in marriage? Tch, tch! An alliance with an enemy household? She was beautiful, yes. But should he become a traitor for that? Impossible! Impossible! Amid all this confusion, Sezhiyan could discover only this much: for some important reason bitterness had arisen between the Pandiyan and Irungovel, and the marriage that ought to have taken place between him and Thamarai had been stopped. Sezhiyan marvelled at the matchless stratagem hidden in Irungovel's words. Why should he not turn that stratagem to the advantage of himself and his country? The question arose in his mind.

<!-- source boundary: scan 153 → scan 154 -->

If he wrote a letter as Irungovel said, it would go to the Pandiyan; once the Pandiyan read it, he would denounce Sezhiyan as a traitor. The invasion would stop. As a result, Sezhiyan's life would be saved; if he somehow escaped death now, he could somehow get away from Irungovel, tell the Pandiyan the truth, and prove that he was not a traitor.

This thought, it must be said, gave Sezhiyan new courage. Along with it came another doubt. Would Irungovel truly agree to give his sister Thamarai to him in marriage? He could ask that too. Thinking that he could probe Irungovel's mind, he looked at him and asked, "I will write the palm leaf to the Pandiyan as you wish. Likewise, will the marriage between Thamarai and me take place?"

Irungovel answered his bold question at once.

"It certainly will. But there is a condition for that too."

"What condition?"

"It is nothing so terrible; I will tell you all that when we finalize it. For now, let us avoid this crisis. What do you say? Will you help? But do not forget that this help concerns your own life as well!"

"I will help; one small condition. You must move me out of this torture chamber and lock me up in some better place. Can you?"

"Certainly! A good place here will be allotted to you. All right, here is the palm leaf—here is the stylus—write at once!"

Receiving the palm leaf and the stylus, Sezhiyan began to write. He wrote down what Irungovel had said in very brief form and handed it to him.

"To Pandiyan Peruvazhuthi, greetings from Sezhiyan. I have heard that you are trying to rescue me from Irungovel. There is no need—I have understood who the bride you kept hidden from me

<!-- source boundary: scan 154 → scan 155 -->

was. Thamarai, Irungovel's sister, and I are to be married. A new relationship with the Velir clan—I am placed in a situation where I must become an enemy of the Pandiyans; forget me.

Sezhiyan"

As he took the palm leaf, Irungovel said, "Bravo!" patted Sezhiyan on the back, and began to laugh loudly. That terrible laughter echoed throughout the dark cave.

"Sezhiyan! You have been thoroughly fooled! This palm leaf will stop the Pandiyan's invasion. But it has no power to stop your life from being lost. I have sent word to Karikalan, putting your life up as the stake. He must return my lost lands. Otherwise Sezhiyan's life will be taken. This, brother, is Irungovel's final warning to the honoured king of Poompuhar. So, brother! One way or another, your life is still hanging in the balance," Irungovel thundered.

Sezhiyan could not contain his fury. Thinking he ought to seize and crush Irungovel, who was making sport of two great kings by using his life, he growled and sprang at him. Irungovel casually struck him down and hurried out. Thamarai went with him. As she left, she turned once to look at Sezhiyan. Gathering all the feelings in his heart onto his tongue, Sezhiyan let out a mighty cry. "Long live the Chola-Pandiyan realm!" That was his cry. Hearing that impassioned battle-song, Thamarai marvelled to herself at Sezhiyan's patriotism. The door closed. Darkness gathered again. Sezhiyan lay in the dark.
