From my youth I have had a special desire to write. In 1939 I wrote a story in my own hand. Its name was "Selva Chandra." That handwritten manuscript, which had been lying somewhere covered in grime, was found recently. As an example of the place that Kaveripoompattinam, now submerged in the sea, had held in my heart even in my youth, I had begun that story as follows:

"In the city called Kaveripoompattinam, in the Dravidian land blessed with water wealth and fertile land ..."

No one should think that only after assuming the office of Chief Minister and governing Tamil Nadu did I newly conceive the idea of creating 'Poompuhar' at Kaveripoompattinam.

The historically renowned places of Tamil Nadu and the names of Tamil heroes who spread fame have been rooted in my heart from my tender years.

It was from those deep roots that this historical imagination arose.

I began 'Romapuri Pandiyan' when "Murasoli" was a weekly. Later Murasoli became a daily newspaper. After that, 'Romapuri Pandiyan' continued in the magazine 'Kumudam'.

<!-- source boundary: scan 6 → scan 7 -->

I had to be careful that history was not distorted by the speed of imagination. I have drawn it not merely as a story but also as a historical portrait of the Tamil land of that time.

My friend Maraimalai Yaan, who stood by me and supplied notes, was of great help. My gratitude and affection will always be there for him, for my dear friend Poet Anandam, who worked to bring this large book into print, for Tamilkani Pathippagam, and for Paari Nilayam, which holds the sales rights.

Tamils who read this have from time to time given me their views and encouraged me. My salutations to them.

I dedicate this book to my beloved father **Muthuvelar**, who savoured every line I wrote, read it with delight, urged others to read it, listened to them, and rejoiced.

With affection,  
M. Karunanidhi

Chennai
