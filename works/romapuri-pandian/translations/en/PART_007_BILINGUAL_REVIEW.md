## English-only corrections newly required by bilingual review

Three E21 corrections were required.

### E21 correction 1 — scan114 / strength after drinking

Verified Tamil:

`அதைக் குடித்துவிட்டுக் கொஞ்சம் தெம்பாகக் கனைத்துக் கொண்டான்.`

Before:

`After drinking it, he cleared his throat and regained a little strength.`

After:

`After drinking it, he cleared his throat with a little more strength.`

Reason: the prior English added a separate result, "regained a little strength," that the source does not independently state. The revision keeps the increased strength attached to the source-visible throat-clearing action.

### E21 correction 2 — scan115 / permission construction

Verified Tamil:

`முத்துவை வீட்டுக்கு அழைத்துவர அண்ணனின் சம்மதம் அவ்வளவு சுலபமாகக் கிடைக்குமென்று அவள் எதிர்பார்க்கவில்லை.`

Before:

`She had never expected her brother's permission to bring Muthu home to come so easily.`

After:

`She had never expected to obtain her brother's permission to bring Muthu home so easily.`

Reason: repairs awkward English while preserving the exact source agency and action.

### E21 correction 3 — scan116 / `பாவை`

Verified Tamil:

`...வீட்டைவிட்டு வெளியேறிய அந்தப் பாவை...`

Before:

`the poor girl who had left home...`

After:

`the maiden who had left home...`

Reason: `பாவை` identifies the maiden figuratively; **poor girl** added an unsupported pity nuance at that point in the sentence.
