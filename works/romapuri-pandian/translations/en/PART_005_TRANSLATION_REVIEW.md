### E16 / Chapter 4 continuation

#### Correction 1

Before:

`she had marked in her heart how a flash of light had appeared and vanished across Thamarai's face.`

After:

`she had taken note in her heart of the flash of light that appeared and vanished across Thamarai's face.`

Reason: improves English syntax while retaining `இதயத்தில் குறித்து வைத்துக் கொண்டாள்` and the source flash-image.

#### Correction 2

Before:

`Could the pounding of the Yavana elder's approaching horse-hooves possibly compete with the sound raised by Muthunagai's own racing heart? It was doubtful!`

After:

`Could the pounding of the Yavana elder's approaching horse-hooves possibly match the sound of Muthunagai's own racing heart? It was doubtful!`

Reason: removes awkward English phrasing while preserving the source's rhetorical comparison between hoofbeats and Muthunagai's heartbeat.

#### Correction 3

Before:

`In a densely wooded place, she and her horse stood without making a sound, and the Yavana elder raced on and lost her.`

After:

`In a densely wooded place, she and her horse stood without making a sound, and the Yavana elder raced past without finding her.`

Reason: renders `ஓடி ஏமாந்து விட்டார்` more naturally in English without changing the narrative event.

### E17 / Chapter 5

#### Correction 4

Before:

`He was middle-aged and powerfully built; whenever he looked at anyone he did so only after summoning an expression of grace to his face.`

After:

`He was middle-aged and powerfully built; whenever he looked at anyone, he had to summon an expression of grace to his face.`

Reason: improves sentence flow while preserving the source contrast between deliberately summoned grace and his naturally stronger valour/majesty.

#### Correction 5

Before:

`The devotee opposed the elder.`

After:

`The devotee confronted the elder.`

Reason: natural English for `கிழவரை அடியார் எதிர்த்தார்` while preserving the immediate action.
