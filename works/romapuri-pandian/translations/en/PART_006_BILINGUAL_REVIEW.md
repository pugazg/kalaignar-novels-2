## English-only corrections newly required by bilingual review

Two corrections were required.

### E18 correction 1 — Irungovel's displayed threat / Sezhiyan status

Verified Tamil:

`பாண்டிய வீரன் செழியன் என்னிடம் தான் அடிமைப்பட்டுக் கிடக்கிறான்.`

Before:

`the Pandiyan warrior Sezhiyan lies enslaved in my custody.`

After:

`the Pandiyan warrior Sezhiyan is being held captive by me.`

Reason: the previous English used **enslaved**, which can imply a stronger institutional condition than the immediate source context requires. **Held captive by me** preserves the verified Tamil sense of Sezhiyan being under Irungovel's control without adding a broader claim.

### E19 correction 2 — tamboolam action

Verified Tamil:

`இருங்கோவேள் தாம்பூலம் கொடுத்தான். அதையும் வாங்கிப் போட்டுக் கொண்டாள்.`

Before:

`Irungovel ... offered her tamboolam. She accepted that too and took it.`

After:

`Irungovel ... offered her tamboolam. She accepted it and put it in her mouth.`

Reason: the previous English did not carry the source-visible action in `போட்டுக் கொண்டாள்`. The revised wording restores that action and preserves the immediate narrative sequence leading into the next sentence about her mouth becoming red.
