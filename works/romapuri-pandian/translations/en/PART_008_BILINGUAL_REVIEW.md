## English-only corrections newly required by bilingual review

Four E22 corrections were required.

### E22 correction 1 — scan122 / arrival question

Verified Tamil:

`நீ என்னய்யா இங்கு வந்தாய்?`

Before:

`What brought you here, man?`

After:

`Why did you come here, man?`

Reason: keeps the source's direct question about Muthunagai's action rather than shifting agency to an external cause.

### E22 correction 2 — scan122 / secret cutting

Verified Tamil:

`திருட்டுத்தனமாக வெட்டுகிறாயா?`

Before:

`Are you stealing timber?`

After:

`Are you cutting wood on the sly?`

Reason: the source asks whether the cutting is being done furtively; the earlier English narrowed it to theft of timber.

### E22 correction 3 — scan128 / palm-leaf report

Verified Tamil:

`உமது ஓலை கண்டு விளக்கமறிந்தோம்.`

Before:

`We have read your palm-leaf and understood its explanation.`

After:

`We have read your palm-leaf and understood the details.`

Reason: removes awkward literal English while retaining the message/report sense without importing external context.

### E22 correction 4 — scan129 / `உத்தமத் தமிழர்`

Verified Tamil:

`செழியன் மிகவும் நல்லவர். உத்தமத் தமிழர்.`

Before:

`Sezhiyan is a very good man, a true Tamil.`

After:

`Sezhiyan is a very good man, a noble Tamil man.`

Reason: preserves the source's evaluative noun phrase without the unnatural English ethnic adjective construction.
